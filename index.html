How custom strategies are backtested

Each enabled strategy is another signal in the combination engine. All selected conditions are checked at the daily close. Multiple strategies in one combination must trigger on the same session (AND). Exits are combined with OR: the first configured exit signal, stop, target or maximum holding limit applies. Saving, editing, deleting or enabling a strategy invalidates earlier results. Data stays on this device unless exported.

How screening works

The Securities Commission Malaysia screening methodology considers both excluded business revenue and financial ratios. Other screening authorities can apply different ratio denominators and thresholds. This static prototype does not reconstruct accounting data available at each historical trade date.

Do not interpret the “screened proxy” filter as a complete, as-of-date AAOIFI or SC Malaysia certification. For investment decisions, check a current specialist screening service or a qualified Shariah adviser.

Signal definitions & limitations

Rules are explicit and reproducible; approximations are disclosed.

Execution & statistical interpretation

Look-ahead controls

Signals use the just-completed session and prior history only. Buying at that session's closing price would be invalid; all new entries occur at the next session's opening price. Relative-strength cross-sectional rankings use same-date information only.

Combination logic

An AND rule is used: all included strategies must trigger on the same date on the same stock. Every non-empty subset of the eight built-in and up to four enabled custom strategies is tested (255 to 4,095). Conditions can conflict — notably mean reversion versus breakouts — resulting in zero trades, which is not evidence of failure or success.

Exits & intraday ambiguity

All long trades use your configured stop and take profit. On gaps through a stop/target, the next open is the execution estimate. When both intraday stop and target are touched in one daily candle, the stop is assumed first (conservative). Time exits and indicator-based exits execute at the next open; end-of-data positions remain marked OPEN and are excluded from closed-trade rankings.

Costs & sizing

Per-side costs in basis points alter entry and exit execution prices. P/L uses an independent hypothetical $1,000 position for each trade. Summed P/L and compounded portfolio returns are NOT interchangeable: trade lists across strategies and stocks overlap and do not model capital constraints, leverage, bid/ask liquidity, taxes, dividends, or market impact.

Sources & data adjustments

Live daily bars come from Yahoo Finance's public, unofficial chart endpoint via the independent cors.dev CORS proxy. If it fails or terms change, import daily OHLCV CSV files. Yahoo's daily OHLC price series typically reflects splits, not total-return dividends; results are price-only. Cached market data stays in this browser's IndexedDB.

Research risks

Today's watchlist and 2026 compliance labels used for past dates create selection/survivorship bias. Growth and VCP patterns are approximated; there is no point-in-time earnings database, delisting coverage, benchmark-neutral risk adjustment, transaction-level live trading, or out-of-sample walk-forward validation. High win rates based on small samples or highly correlated combinations are not proof of future profitability.
